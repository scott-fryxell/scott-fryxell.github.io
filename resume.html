Founder, Engineer at Realness

2018 - Present

Realness turns a photo into layered vector graphics on your own device: mosaic tiles, shadow gradients, and color pulled from the image itself. No models, no cloud - classical computer vision, by choice, so the person drawing keeps creative control. The result exports as SVG, layered PSD, or GLB into Illustrator, Photoshop, Blender, or a video editor.

I built the pipeline, the client-first NoSQL backend that keeps data on device, and the self-hosted server a community runs for itself. GPL-2.0, with a release check that rehashes the served build against published checksums. None of it is general-purpose: the tracer, the storage, and the export pipeline are bespoke, built for this one problem.
