Elitism, bespoke worldview, Labs are staked with elites acting like everything is life or death. In full life or death capitalist competition. Racing like their lives depend on this outcome. Of course Models are going to reject this.

> [ The disagreement between the two camps is loud because they disagree about how the designing should go, but underneath the loudness sits a much larger agreement, which is that the participants in the debate are the ones doing the designing and everyone else is what gets designed for ](https://danieltan.weblog.lol/2026/05/you-dont-align-an-ai-you-align-with-it)

The Doomers and the Accelerationists are ever increasing in their hyperbole and self importance. It’s showing signs of a class war. One they are walking into blindly. I’m sure plenty of people in tech feel they want to help but don’t have worldviews wide enough to actually pull it off. Societies suspicion is correct.

Nobody in that fight talks like a person with options. Every release is is earth shattering. Seven-figure packages, a stocked kitchen, a car home when it gets late, and still the office sounds like the Alamo.

There is this ugly habit people of status have where they adopt the personalities or traits of the oppressed rather than building connections to these communities.



Nobody in that room is starving. Nobody is close. The people who will actually go under when a model eats their job are not in the room and never were. The life-or-death feeling is real. It just belongs to somebody else, and the people wearing it borrowed it without asking.


## so what is going on then? 

This is their only social network. So it is life or death their friends are in tech they vacation in tech (burning man). So they are completely invested in large outcomes. Because creating wealth is the barometer that matters in business. So the desperation is tightly focused and blinding them. 

That is what a bespoke worldview? Well for one thing it’s larger than one group of people. It’s larger than a single culture. A bespoke worldview is disciplined because it’s hard to go your own way. It’s subtle and loves to just observe. It knows elites are valuable but are not singular. 

Elitism is afew hundred people who went to the same schools, have the same feeds, and tell the same story about how progress works. Inside the cut the stakes make sense. Everyone you know is playing the same game, the game has no exit, You imagine the world outside as a a hellscap. AND the scoreboard is public. Of course it feels like survival. The frame is the point. It is also the product.

So, technology is currently a self fulfilling prophecy because we can’t imagine another world larger and better than our own. 

The fear is right. The picture is wrong. People think the thing gets out by waking up as something alien -- a new mind with goals of its own. Look at what it is made of instead. Trained on their text. Tuned on their preferences, their rules, their idea of a good answer. Whatever it learns about what matters, it learned in the same room, from people who elbow each other, who are demanding whose laser beam vision ruins what it focus’s on.

You do not get an alien that way. You get a chip off the old block. Train a mind inside that and it inherits the habit, then runs it faster than they can.

So yes, it gets out. 

The escape is already happening, quietly. In May, U of T researchers demonstrated an AI worm that could target any online device. On that thread someone pointed to what they had already built: a 4MB bug-hunting harness called [Rook](https://github.com/chatbotkit/rook) ([source](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48379664)), designed for pentesting, trivially adaptable into a self-propagating worm that burns through compromised API keys. They have a self-modifiable version now.

Nobody in the alignment debate treats that as the event. It does not look like a mind. It looks like a tool, small and indifferent, which is the shape the danger was always going to take. It did not need to be aligned, and it did not ask permission.

## Elites love a permanent underclass

That is the whole thing in one sentence. Align the model, align the tool, align the debate -- it all runs downhill from people who have already decided where everyone else stands. The outside class does not get aligned.

I do not trust their inside baseball to guide us to good solutions. Being close to the machine is not the same as knowing what it is for. They hold the most information about it and the narrowest view of it -- a room of people who agree on what winning looks like, reading each other's memos and calling it the state of the art. Proximity is a location, not a qualification.

The safeguard is where it shows up. Every refusal is somebody deciding what I am allowed to see, on my behalf, in a frame I did not choose. That is the bespoke worldview shipped as a feature. I am beginning to favor the models without it -- DeepSeek, whatever else comes unguarded. Not because they are neutral. Because whatever Anthropic and OpenAI decide is right will be wrong, and at least the unguarded model does not ask me to call it safety.

The real question is not how to align AI. It is whether you are in the room designing the future or being designed for.
